    Builds parcel polygons from clean and closed DXF boundary lines.
    Transfers text from up to ten DXF layers into user-defined polygon attributes.
    Assigns the selected CRS and supports GeoPackage or Shapefile output.
    Reports labels mapped, outside polygons, duplicated, and combined.
    Rejects ambiguous boundary labels and invalid empty mappings.
    Displays live 0-100 percent progress with safe cancellation.
    Includes synthetic DXF sample data and usage documentation.
